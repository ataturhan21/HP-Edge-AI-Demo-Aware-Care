# Aware Care solution brief

“A local AI healthcare demo on HP ZGX, designed for future deployment at the healthcare edge.”

Aware Care is a responsive web demonstration for healthcare CIOs and CTOs. A fictional adult describes a concern, optionally confirms relevant profile/record facts, answers up to 10 relevant questions, reviews the case, and explores guidance, supporting evidence, explained provider matches and a downloadable summary. Reviewed urgent or unsupported scenarios interrupt the normal journey.

The Nano hosts the application, local storage, processing and ZRT inference. The presenter can explain actual mode, source freshness, local boundaries, measured latency and failure recovery. Mock mode is a complete synthetic simulation. Local mode is a separately verified ZRT integration and never silently substitutes mocked or cloud-generated results.

Potential benefits include fewer wrong-destination appointments, less repetitive intake, more appropriate use of clinician capacity, reduced patient navigation effort and lower avoidable episode spending. These remain hypotheses. No saved click, simulated visit or downloaded PDF is counted as clinical benefit, avoided utilization or insurer savings.

The product master preserves B01-B08 and their measurement definitions. The demo can measure workflow completion, response times, source/claim checks, provider-filter correctness, deletion and network behavior. A healthcare pilot is required for actual health, workload and spending outcomes.

Customer presentation: 1 minute concern/privacy, 1 minute record review/questions with transparent prefill, 1 minute guidance/evidence, 1 minute provider fit/summary, 1 minute measured runtime/reset. Rehearse urgent interruption separately. Native apps, direct booking, EHR writes and unrestricted post-result medical chat are future scope.
